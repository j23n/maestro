Five alternatives to Tito

Same line style and palette, so any of them drops into the screens.
Moka
A stovetop coffee pot
Found in nearly every Italian kitchen. Puffs a little steam when you get something right and cools off on rest days.
Lina
A lemon from the Amalfi coast
The sunniest of the five. Easy to recognise at any size, and its leaf droops when it's thinking.
Romeo
A street cat from Rome
Rome's ruins are famous for their cats. Laid-back and a little lazy, he curls up in the margin and nudges new words your way.
Lucia
A firefly; her name means light
The reading-first pick. Her glow could literally light up new words in a story and fade as you learn them.
Rina
A swallow who comes back each spring
A picture of spaced review: she flies off and returns right when a word is due. Her terracotta throat is real, not invented.
