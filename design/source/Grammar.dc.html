Did you spot it? Giulia says un cappuccino, but una birra.

un and una

Every Italian noun is masculine or feminine. Both words mean "a", and the noun decides which one you use.

Masculine
un caffè
un cappuccino
un cornetto
Feminine
una birra
una pizza
una spremuta
Tip: most nouns ending in -o are masculine and most ending in -a are feminine. Others, like caffè, are easiest to learn with their article.
