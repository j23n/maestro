The method

How Maestro teaches

Not a chatbot. Not a textbook. A structured curriculum that responds to how you learn.

S

Scenarios from real life

Every lesson is set in a real-world scenario — ordering at a bar, navigating a train station, making plans with friends. You learn grammar and vocabulary in context, the way language actually works.

T

Targets your weak spots

Maestro tracks exactly which grammar points you’ve mastered and which still trip you up. It builds lessons around your gaps, revisits vocabulary at the right moments, and adjusts as you improve.

F

Feedback that explains

Every answer gets a score and an explanation — not just right or wrong, but why, with links to the relevant grammar rule and tips on spelling and usage.

Personalized for you

Your lessons, in your world

Tell Maestro who you are, and your scenarios will match your life. Same grammar. Your world.

✈️ The traveller

Airports, hotels, train stations, asking for directions. Practical language for navigating daily life with confidence.

🍝 The food lover

Markets, restaurants, cooking vocabulary, regional dishes. Grammar through the language of food and culture.

📚 The student

Universities, libraries, academic discussion. Formal register and the vocabulary of learning and ideas.

🎭 The culture enthusiast

Art, history, cinema, music. A gateway to centuries of literature, film, and culture.

Beyond interests, Maestro personalizes every lesson to your current level and grammar history. It knows which grammar points you’ve already mastered — and which ones to practise next.

A lesson

A lesson in Maestro

Pick your time, and Maestro builds a lesson that fits.

01

Pick your time

Pick your pace — Maestro builds a lesson that fits. More time means more variety: reading passages, writing exercises, grammar deep-dives.

02

Review vocabulary

While your lesson loads, you review vocabulary flashcards with spaced repetition. Cards you know well fade away; tricky ones keep coming back until they stick.

03

Practice with drills

Fill in blanks, correct errors, write freely. Each drill is tied to a real scenario and specific grammar points. Your answers are scored instantly with clear explanations.

04

Read, write, speak

Longer activities build fluency: read a passage in your target language and answer questions, write responses to prompts and get grammar feedback, practice conversation starters.

05

See your progress

After each lesson: a summary of what you practised, where you struggled, and detailed feedback. Your profile updates with new grammar mastery, vocabulary gains, and scenario progress.
