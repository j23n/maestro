Il metodo

How Maestro teaches

Not a chatbot. Not a textbook. A structured curriculum that responds to how you learn.

S

Scenario-based curriculum

Every lesson is set in a real-world scenario — ordering at a bar, navigating a train station, making plans with friends. You learn grammar and vocabulary in context, the way language actually works.

A

Adapts to you

Maestro tracks what you know and what trips you up. It picks scenarios that target your weak grammar, revisits vocabulary at optimal intervals, and adjusts as you improve.

F

AI-powered feedback

Every answer gets evaluated by AI that understands Italian grammar. Not just right or wrong — you get explanations of why, links to grammar rules, and tips on accents and spelling.

Una lezione

A lesson in Maestro

Pick your time, and Maestro builds a lesson that fits.

01

Pick your time

Pick your pace — Maestro builds a lesson that fits. More time means more variety: reading passages, writing exercises, grammar deep-dives.

02

Review vocabulary

While your lesson generates in the background, you review vocabulary flashcards with spaced repetition. Cards you know well fade away; tricky ones keep coming back.

03

Practice with drills

Fill in blanks, correct errors, write freely. Each drill is tied to a real scenario and specific grammar points. AI scores your answers instantly and explains mistakes.

04

Read, write, speak

Longer activities build fluency: read an Italian passage and answer questions, write responses to prompts and get grammar feedback, practice conversation starters.

05

See your progress

After each lesson: a summary of what you practiced, where you struggled, and AI-generated feedback. Your profile updates with new grammar mastery and scenario depth.
