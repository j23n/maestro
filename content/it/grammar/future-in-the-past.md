## When it's used

When you report, from a past point of view, something that was still in the future: *Mio padre decise che saremmo andati a Bologna.* (My father decided we would go to Bologna.)

English uses the simple conditional ("would go"). Italian uses the **condizionale passato**, even if the event really happened later: *Sapevo che sarebbe arrivato* (I knew he would arrive).

## How it's formed

Past verb (passato prossimo, imperfetto, passato remoto) + *che* + condizionale passato.

| Past verb | Future in the past |
| --- | --- |
| Ha detto | che sarebbe venuta. |
| Pensavo | che avrebbero capito. |
| Decise | che saremmo andati a Bologna. |

## Watch out

*Ha detto che verrebbe* (simple conditional) is a common mistake, influenced by English and other languages. In standard Italian it means something different: that she would come under some condition.
