title: Object pronouns
level: A2
summary: lo, la, li, le (it, them) and gli, le (to him, to her) before the verb.
order: 90
---
## When it's used

To avoid repeating a noun: *Hai visto il film? Sì, l'ho visto.*

## How it's formed

| | Direct | Indirect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| me | mi | mi |
| you | ti | ti |
| him, it | lo | gli |
| her, it | la | le |
| you (Lei) | La | Le |
| us | ci | ci |
| you (plural) | vi | vi |
| them | li, le | gli |

The pronoun goes before a conjugated verb and attaches to an infinitive, gerund or *tu* imperative: *Voglio vederlo.* *Guardala!*

## Watch out

In the passato prossimo the participle agrees with *lo*, *la*, *li*, *le*: *Le ho viste* (I saw them, feminine).
