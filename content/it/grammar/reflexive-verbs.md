## When it's used

When the action falls back on the subject (*mi lavo*, I wash), for many daily routines (*mi alzo alle sette*) and for verbs that are simply reflexive in Italian (*ricordarsi*, *accorgersi*).

## How it's formed

The pronoun goes before the conjugated verb, or attaches to an infinitive, gerund or imperative.

| | alzarsi |
| --- | --- |
| io | mi alzo |
| tu | ti alzi |
| lui, lei | si alza |
| noi | ci alziamo |
| voi | vi alzate |
| loro | si alzano |

Compound tenses take **essere**, and the participle agrees: *Marta si è alzata presto.*

Infinitive: *Devo alzarmi* or *Mi devo alzare*. Both are correct.
