A2 adds the dative in full, two-way prepositions, reflexive verbs, the Präteritum of *sein*, *haben* and the modals, comparison, adjective endings after articles and the first Konjunktiv II (*würde*, *hätte*, *wäre*, *könnte*). Subordinate clauses with *weil*, *dass*, *wenn* and *ob* put the verb at the end. Dialogue and messages carry the Perfekt; short narratives can use the Präteritum.
