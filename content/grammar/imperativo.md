## When it's used

To tell someone to do something, give directions or make an offer: *Prendi un caffè!* *Girate a destra.*

## How it's formed

| | guardare | prendere | sentire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tu | guarda | prendi | senti |
| noi | guardiamo | prendiamo | sentiamo |
| voi | guardate | prendete | sentite |
| Lei | guardi | prenda | senta |

The negative for **tu** is **non** + infinitive: *non andare*, *non mangiare*. Other persons just add *non*: *non guardate*.

## Common irregulars

Short *tu* forms: *va'* (andare), *fa'* (fare), *da'* (dare), *sta'* (stare), *di'* (dire). Pronouns attach to them and double the consonant: *dammi*, *fallo*, *dimmelo*.
