## When it's used

For statements about people in general, where English says "you", "people" or uses the passive: *In Italia si mangia tardi.* (In Italy people eat late.) *Come si dice "train"?*

## How it's formed

**si** + third person verb. With a plural noun the verb is plural (this is the *si passivante*): *Si vendono biglietti* (Tickets are sold).

- With a reflexive verb, *si si* becomes **ci si**: *Ci si alza presto.*
- Adjectives after *si è* are plural: *Si è contenti quando...*
- Compound tenses take **essere**: *Si è parlato molto.*
