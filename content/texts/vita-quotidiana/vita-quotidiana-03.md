# Quando ero piccola
level: A2
collection: vita-quotidiana
order: 3
topics: childhood, family
introduces: imperfetto

Quando ero piccola, vivevo in un paese vicino al mare. || When I was little, I lived in a village near the sea.
La nostra casa era piccola, ma aveva un balcone pieno di fiori. || Our house was small, but it had a balcony full of flowers.
Ogni mattina mia nonna mi accompagnava a scuola a piedi. || Every morning my grandmother walked me to school.
Il pomeriggio giocavo in strada con i bambini dei vicini. || In the afternoon I played in the street with the neighbours' children.
D'estate andavamo in spiaggia quasi ogni giorno. || In summer we went to the beach almost every day.
Mio padre lavorava fino a tardi e lo vedevamo solo a cena. || My father worked until late and we only saw him at dinner.
La domenica, però, ci portava sempre a mangiare il gelato. || On Sundays, though, he always took us out for ice cream.
Io prendevo sempre fragola e limone. || I always had strawberry and lemon.
Adesso vivo in città, ma quando sento l'odore del mare penso a quegli anni. || Now I live in the city, but when I smell the sea I think of those years.
